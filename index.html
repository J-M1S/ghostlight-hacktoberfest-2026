    A NOTE ON TRUST

    Small, open, and yours.

    Search runs in your browser using the open-weight all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model and Transformers.js. Your note text isn't sent to an inference server by this app. The site, fonts, library, and model are downloaded from hosting and third-party providers, which can see normal network information such as your IP address.

    Fragments are stored in this browser, not synced. Clearing browser data may erase them: export a backup. Similarity is a suggestion, not proof that two memories match. Long fragments are searched in overlapping passages.

    Made for Hacktoberfest's “Ghost in the Machine.” Fictional examples. Please don't enter secrets or sensitive details on a shared device.
